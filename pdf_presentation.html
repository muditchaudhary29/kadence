The Core Problem

Why Candidates Struggle in High-Stakes Interviews

⚠️ Uncontrolled Pacing & Velocity

Nerves cause candidates to speak too fast (>170 WPM) or pause unnaturally, making core points hard to follow.

⚡ Excessive Filler Word Dilution

Frequent hesitation terms ("um", "like", "basically", "you know") erode perceived authority and technical confidence.

📐 Unstructured STAR Framework

Candidates fail to structure stories around Situation, Task, Action, & Result (STAR), missing key impact metrics.

📄 Generic & Unfocused Practice

Generic question banks fail to prepare candidates for specific resume projects, technical notes, or job descriptions.

Feature Deep-Dive

Real-Time Vocal Telemetry & Visual Analytics

⚡ Speed & Filler Telemetry

  • ► WPM Arc Gauge: Tracks speaking pace against optimal 120–160 WPM sweet spot.
  • ► Filler Word Engine: Detects "um", "like", "basically", "you know" with count & ratio.
  • ► Live Pills: Dynamic visual pill markers highlighting filler locations.
  • ► Confidence Index: Continuously computes vocal stability score (0-100%).

🎯 STAR Framework Radar

  • ► Situation (25%): Evaluates background context and team setup.
  • ► Task (25%): Validates problem definition & engineering objective.
  • ► Action (25%): Assesses personal execution & technical details.
  • ► Result (25%): Scans for quantitative metrics (% latency, revenue).
